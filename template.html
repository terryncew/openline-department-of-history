3-AXIS SOURCE INSPECTOR
HOW THIS DEPARTMENT WORKS

A historical comparison has to earn its place.

Honest Evidence Boundary: This engine searches five curated historical inquiries (42 sources, 104 core records, 15 comparable argument chains). It classifies every citizen question as Supported, Partially Supported (with missing coverage explicitly disclosed), or Unsupported (refusing future predictions, ideological laws, and uncurated topics rather than inventing citations).

Comparable Historical Arguments: For every precedent, the lab separates what contemporaries argued at the time and what policymakers expected to happen from what subsequent events supported or contradicted and what later historians concluded.

Three-Axis Evidence Separation: Every source is checked separately for (1) Byte Integrity (SHA-256 card/PDF checksum, which proves only that local bytes were not altered), (2) Provenance Standing (Primary record, Institutional data, Full/Excerpt Scholarship, or Abstract/Synopsis Only), and (3) Evidentiary Standing (Available, Reopen on Challenge, Disputed, or Withdrawn).

Public research preview: This is an independent open-source research tool, not an official government institution. Automated acceptance tests were performed in headless Chromium; no independent human comprehension study has occurred, so whether first-time readers understand the site is unproven.

Consequential Correction: When a material source is challenged or withdrawn locally, conclusions and arguments relying solely on that source are quarantined, while multi-source conclusions narrow to what their surviving independent sources can support.
