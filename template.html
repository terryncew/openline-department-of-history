HOW THIS DEPARTMENT WORKS

Serious history, explained clearly—with every source open to challenge.

1. A Story-First Journey at Three Depths: Every historical inquiry can be read as a 1-minute Quick Look, an interactive 6-step Explore journey (The Question → Back Then → The Debate → What Happened → What’s Different Today → Explore the Evidence), or a full Examine the Evidence research workbench. All three depths pull from the exact same verified sources.

2. Honest Coverage Boundaries: This library covers five curated historical inquiries (44 sources, 105 core records, 15 comparable argument chains). When you ask a question, it tells you honestly whether your question is Supported, Partially Supported (showing what is missing), or Unsupported (refusing future predictions and uncurated topics rather than making up answers).

3. What People Expected vs. What Actually Happened: History is lived forward, not backward. In The Debate, you can inspect what contemporaries argued and expected at the time before revealing what actually happened afterward and how later historians disagreed.

4. Three-Axis Verification & Consequential Correction: Every source is checked separately for (1) File Integrity (SHA-256 checksum, proving local bytes were not altered), (2) Source Type (Primary record, Institutional data, Full Scholarship, or Abstract/Synopsis Only), and (3) Current Standing. If you challenge or withdraw a source in your browser notebook, claims relying solely on that source are quarantined, while multi-source claims automatically narrow to their surviving independent sources.
