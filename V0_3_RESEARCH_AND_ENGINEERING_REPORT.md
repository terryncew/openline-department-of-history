## 1. Authorized Research Intervention: Standards & Historical Methods Consulted

In `v0.1.0` and `v0.2.0`, asking about any historical subject outside the 5 curated inquiries (such as *"the history of espionage"*, *"the 1918 influenza pandemic"*, *"the printing press"*, or *"the 1930s Dust Bowl"*) returned a terminal `UNSUPPORTED (OUTSIDE_CURATED_EVIDENCE_LIBRARY)` refusal card. While honest about boundaries, that refusal acted as a dead end for a curious learner. Conversely, conventional LLM or uncurated web workflows answer unfamiliar historical questions by blending verified primary facts, contested historiographical opinions, and unverified popular myths into a single confident prose stream without provenance controls or promotion gates.

To resolve this engineering gap without paid APIs or hallucinated certainty, we consulted three foundational standards and historical research frameworks:

1. **W3C PROV-DM Provenance Data Model ([W3C Recommendation, PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) & [PROV-Overview](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/))**:
   - Models provenance as explicit bindings between **Entities** (source documents, citation cards, and historical claims), **Activities** (ingestion, provisional synthesis, claim correction, source challenge, and promotion review), and **Agents** (curators, reviewers, and archival institutions).
   - **Engineering Application in v0.3**: Every provisional inquiry tracks its full lifecycle in `inquiry.reviewAuditTrail` (`PROVISIONAL_INQUIRY_GENERATED` → `PROVISIONAL_SOURCE_STANDING_CHANGE` → `PROVISIONAL_CLAIM_CORRECTED_AND_VERIFIED` / `PROVISIONAL_CLAIM_QUARANTINED` → `PROMOTED_TO_VERIFIED_LOCAL`) and issues a deterministic, SHA-256-hashed promotion receipt (`openline-provisional-promotion-receipt.v1`) only after all provenance bindings are satisfied.
2. **SAA–ACRL/RBMS Guidelines for Primary Source Literacy ([Society of American Archivists & Association of College and Research Libraries, 2018](https://www2.archivists.org/sites/all/files/GuidelinesForPrimarySourceLiteracy-June2018.pdf))**:
   - Articulates core archival competencies: distinguishing primary contemporaneous records from secondary syntheses (Conceptualize 1A), identifying material gaps and silences in archival collections (Read, Understand, and Summarize 3A–3C), and factoring institutional production context into evidentiary weight (Interpret, Analyze, and Evaluate 4A–4C).
   - **Engineering Application in v0.3**: Built `Engine.classifyEpistemicStatus()` and `Engine.summarizeEpistemicBreakdown()` to enforce a strict three-tier epistemic separation across every claim and source:
     - **Tier 1 — `VERIFIED_EVIDENCE`**: Claims bound to primary archival or official institutional records (`PRIMARY_ARCHIVAL_RECORD` or `INSTITUTIONAL_RECORD`) with verified HTTPS archive URLs, exact document locators, passing SHA-256 card hashes, and unchallenged standing.
     - **Tier 2 — `DISPUTED_INTERPRETATION`**: Competing contemporary arguments (`historicalArguments`) and rival scholarly frameworks (`interpretations`) with distinct causal emphases—always kept plural so consensus is never manufactured.
     - **Tier 3 — `UNVERIFIED_MATERIAL`**: Unreviewed provisional claims (`PENDING_REVIEW`), popular historical myths awaiting primary-locator correction, claims resting solely on `ABSTRACT_ONLY` / `SYNOPSIS_ONLY` secondary previews, or claims whose sole supporting source is challenged/withdrawn.
3. **Stanford History Education Group / Digital Inquiry Group Historical Thinking Protocol ([Digital Inquiry Group Historical Thinking Chart](https://www.inquirygroup.org/history-lessons/historical-thinking-chart) & Sam Wineburg, [Library of Congress Teaching with Primary Sources](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/teachers/about-this-program/teaching-with-primary-sources-partner-program/documents/historical_thinking.pdf))**:
   - Requires **Sourcing** (who wrote this, when, and for what purpose?), **Contextualization** (what constraints shaped decision-makers before the outcome was known?), and **Corroboration** (comparing multiple independent accounts and primary vs. secondary records before accepting a claim).
   - **Engineering Application in v0.3**: Every generated provisional inquiry includes an intentional `UNVERIFIED_MATERIAL` claim representing a widespread popular misconception on that topic, paired with a primary-source corroboration prompt and a Step 6 **Source-Investigation Challenge** comparing primary archival records against secondary summaries.

## 2. How the v0.3 Five-Stage System Works

### Requirement 1: Recognize When an Inquiry Falls Outside Verified Coverage Instead of Silently Failing
- `Engine.detectUnfamiliarHistoricalSubject(questionText, data)` and `Engine.askHistory(questionText, data)` evaluate every natural-language query:
  - If the query matches one of the 5 core verified inquiries (`cholera`, `triangle`, `banking`, `hormuz`, `israel-palestine`), it routes to the verified dossier (`SUPPORTED` or `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`).
  - If the query is a **non-historical future prediction** (`"What will happen in 2030?"`), **universal ideological determinism** (`"Has history proved that one political ideology always succeeds?"`), or a **non-historical keyword trap** (`"campfire on a river bank"`), it fails closed (`UNSUPPORTED`, `isResearchableHistoricalSubject: false`).
  - If the query is a **genuine historical subject outside the 5 core verified inquiries** (such as `"the history of espionage"`, `"How did U.S. cities respond to the 1918 influenza pandemic?"`, `"What is the history of the printing press?"`, `"What caused the 1930s Dust Bowl?"`, or any custom historical question like `"What caused the fall of the Roman Republic in the first century BCE?"`), `askHistory` preserves the honest core boundary (`status: 'UNSUPPORTED'`, `boundaryCode: 'OUTSIDE_CURATED_EVIDENCE_LIBRARY'`, `outsideVerifiedCoverage: true`) **and** attaches a structured `researchOffer` (`isResearchableHistoricalSubject: true`).

### Requirement 2: Offer to Research the New Subject Using Accessible Historical Sources
- In the Front Door Question Console (`#question-briefing`), instead of a dead-end red refusal banner, the UI renders the **Unfamiliar Subject Recognition & Research Offer Panel (`#provisional-research-offer`)**:
  - Explains clearly why the topic falls outside the 5 core verified inquiries,
  - Previews the accessible primary, institutional, and scholarly sources (`accessibleSourcesPreview`) with their archive URLs, locators, and coverage depth (`FULL_TEXT`, `PRIMARY_EXCERPT`, `SYNOPSIS_ONLY`),
  - Provides a 1-click button (`#launch-provisional-research-btn`) to generate and open the provisional inquiry.

### Requirement 3 & 4: Generate a Provisional, Explorable Inquiry Separating Verified Evidence, Disputed Interpretations, and Unverified Material
- `Engine.researchHistoricalSubject(queryOrKey, options)` generates a complete provisional inquiry (`inquiryStatus: 'PROVISIONAL'`, `promotedToVerified: false`) that plugs directly into the 6-step Story Journey (`The Question → Back Then → The Debate → What Happened → What’s Different Today → Explore the Evidence`) and all 3 reading depths (`Quick Look` ~1 min, `Explore`, `Examine the Evidence`).
- For topics outside both the 5 core inquiries and the 4 pre-bundled researched packs, `Engine.researchHistoricalSubject()` builds a non-hallucinating **Guided Open-Access Archival Scaffold (`GUIDED_CUSTOM_SCAFFOLD`)** connected to 6 verified open-access primary repositories ([Library of Congress Digital Collections](https://www.loc.gov/collections/), [U.S. National Archives Catalog](https://catalog.archives.gov/), [Yale Avalon Project](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/), [UK National Archives Research Guides](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/), [Smithsonian Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess), and [Digital Inquiry Group](https://www.inquirygroup.org/history-lessons/historical-thinking-chart)).

### Requirement 5: Review, Correction, Source Challenges, and the Promotion Gate
- Inside the **Provisional Review & Promotion Workbench (`#provisional-review-banner`)** and `Engine.promoteProvisionalInquiry(inquiry, overrides, {reviewerNote})`, promotion from `PROVISIONAL` to `VERIFIED_LOCAL` is **blocked (`PROMOTION_BLOCKED`)** if any of six verification safeguards fail:
  1. `UNREVIEWED_PROVISIONAL_MATERIAL`: Any `UNVERIFIED_MATERIAL` / `PENDING_REVIEW` claim remains unreviewed (must be corrected & bound to a verified primary locator via `Engine.correctProvisionalClaim` or excluded via `Engine.quarantineProvisionalClaim`).
  2. `FABRICATED_OR_INVALID_CITATION`: Any source has a non-HTTPS, placeholder (`example.com`, `localhost`), or malformed URL, lacks an exact locator, or fails SHA-256 citation-card verification.
  3. `HALLUCINATION_OR_RECORD_VALIDATION_FAILURE`: Any claim or argument contains an unverified direct quotation or smuggles a verbatim quote onto an `ABSTRACT_ONLY` / `SYNOPSIS_ONLY` source.
  4. `MISLEADING_OR_UNBOUNDED_ANALOGY`: Any comparison omits an explicit breakdown limit (`< 15` chars), makes a deterministic/single-cause overclaim, or triggers domain-specific structural analogy errors.
  5. `MISSING_COMPETING_INTERPRETATIONS` / `MANUFACTURED_CONSENSUS_ERROR`: Fewer than 2 competing scholarly interpretations with distinct causal emphases or missing counterexamples.
  6. `ACTIVE_SOURCE_WITHDRAWAL_HOLD`: Any source in the inquiry is currently challenged (`WITHDRAWN`, `DISPUTED`, or `REVISED`) without resolution.
