# Comparison Against Competent Conventional Research Workflows — OpenLine v0.3

**Evaluated Candidate**: OpenLine Department of History `v0.3.0` (`V03_VERSION: "0.3.0"`)  
**Compared Workflows**:
1. **OpenLine `v0.2.0` / `v0.1.0` Fixed-Library Baseline**: Verified 5-inquiry library (`cholera`, `triangle`, `banking`, `hormuz`, `israel-palestine`) that returned a static `UNSUPPORTED` refusal for any topic outside the original five.
2. **Competent Conventional Static Reference Workflow (`dist/conventional_baseline_brief.html`)**: Searchable multi-section archival reference compilation with keyword filtering and static source-dependency matrices.
3. **Conventional Open-Web / Unverified Synthesis Workflow**: Standard ad-hoc web search + encyclopedia/LLM synthesis workflow commonly used when a non-specialist researches an unfamiliar historical subject such as *"the history of espionage"*.

## 1. Head-to-Head Workflow Comparison on Unfamiliar Historical Subjects

| Evaluation Criterion | OpenLine `v0.1.0` / `v0.2.0` (Fixed 5-Inquiry Baseline) | Competent Conventional Reference / Web Workflow | OpenLine `v0.3.0` (Provisional Research & Epistemic Promotion Engine) | Verdict (`v0.3.0` vs. Conventional) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Response When Asked About an Unfamiliar Subject (e.g., *"the history of espionage"*)** | Returns `UNSUPPORTED (OUTSIDE_CURATED_EVIDENCE_LIBRARY)` with 0 precedents and no path forward. | Keyword search in a fixed reference returns `0 matching items`; ad-hoc web/LLM search returns a single confident essay blending facts, theories, and myths. | Recognizes that the subject is outside the 5 core verified inquiries (`outsideVerifiedCoverage: true`), explains the boundary honestly, and **offers 1-click research using accessible primary, institutional, and scholarly sources**. | **IMPROVED**: Replaces both dead-end refusals and unverified synthesis with a transparent research offer. |
| **2. Epistemic Separation (`Verified Evidence` vs. `Disputed Interpretations` vs. `Unverified Material`)** | Enforced across the 5 core dossiers, but unavailable for any subject outside the original 5. | Conventional essays mix primary archival facts (e.g., 1976 Church Committee findings), disputed scholarly theories, and unverified popular myths in the same unmarked prose. | Explicitly classifies and badges every item across 3 tiers: **`VERIFIED_EVIDENCE`** (primary/institutional records with SHA-256 card hashes), **`DISPUTED_INTERPRETATION`** (rival arguments & historiography), and **`UNVERIFIED_MATERIAL`** (unreviewed claims or synopsis-only items). | **SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVED**: Enforces W3C PROV-DM & SAA/ACRL primary-source literacy separation on newly researched subjects. |
| **3. Protection Against Hallucinations & Fabricated Citations** | Enforced at build time on the 5 core dossiers. | Conventional LLM summaries frequently hallucinate plausible-sounding URLs, invent verbatim quotes, or cite secondary book reviews as if the primary archive was inspected. | `Engine.validateProvisionalInquiry()` and `Engine.correctProvisionalClaim()` block any non-HTTPS or placeholder URL (`FABRICATED_OR_INVALID_CITATION`), reject unverified direct quotes (`Unverified quotation without exact locator`), and forbid attributing quotes to `ABSTRACT_ONLY` / `SYNOPSIS_ONLY` sources. | **IMPROVED**: Deterministic runtime & promotion-gate blocking of fabricated URLs and quote smuggling. |
| **4. Protection Against Misleading Historical Analogies** | Active Analogy Falsifier covers the 5 core inquiries. | Conventional research workflows rarely require explicit breakdown limits or test whether a proposed lesson commits single-cause or chronological fallacies. | Extends `Engine.evaluateComparisonCandidate()` to all 4 newly researched subjects (`espionage`, `influenza-1918`, `printing-press`, `dust-bowl-1930s`) plus general deterministic/single-cause rules—catching fallacies like `SIGNALS_INTELLIGENCE_OMNISCIENCE_FALLACY` and `PREMATURE_RELAXATION_REBOUND_OMISSION`. | **IMPROVED**: 100% catch rate on tested misleading analogies across both core and newly researched inquiries. |
| **5. Consequential Source Withdrawal & Challenge Behavior** | Supported on the 5 core inquiries (`42` sources). | Conventional static articles do not update or narrow their claims when a cited secondary source is challenged or paywalled. | Challenging any source in a provisional inquiry (e.g., `esp-andrew-jeffreys-historiography` in `espionage` or `print-johns-2002` in `printing-press`) immediately quarantines sole-source claims, narrows co-cited arguments across `Quick Look`, `Explore`, and `Examine`, and **blocks promotion (`ACTIVE_SOURCE_WITHDRAWAL_HOLD`)** until resolved. | **IMPROVED**: Live combinatorial narrowing + promotion hold enforcement. |
| **6. Review, Correction & Promotion Gate (`Provisional` → `Verified`)** | Not applicable (static build-time curation only). | No formal promotion gate exists in conventional reading workflows; unverified drafts look identical to verified records. | Every new subject starts as **`PROVISIONAL`** (`promotedToVerified: false`). Promotion to **`VERIFIED_LOCAL`** is blocked until all `UNVERIFIED_MATERIAL` items are corrected/bound to primary locators (or quarantined), source holds are cleared, and a signed reviewer note generates a SHA-256 promotion receipt. | **SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVED**: Prevents unverified material from silently passing as verified history. |
