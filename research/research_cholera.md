## Boundaries for implementation

Keep facts, analogy prompts, scholarly interpretations and unmeasured effects visibly distinct. Present-side cells are questions, not sourced claims about a named current emergency. A source withdrawn from standing must reopen dependent claims; do not silently switch to a different source. Do not turn “pump closure was reasonable” into “pump closure ended the epidemic.” No precise lives-saved estimate is supported here.

The two competing scholarly positions are Oleckno’s qualified assessment of a durable evidential contribution and Koch’s more critical assessment of adequacy and retrospective hero-making. They are not a fabricated dispute about whether contaminated water can transmit cholera. Brody et al. supply a third, methodological reading of the map narrative.
